Ronny, thanks for raising this. We did not directly benchmark open-source frameworks in the IJHE paper. I have now run a separate, reproducible seven-species equilibrium check with a SciPy Gibbs minimizer and Cantera's multiphase solver, retaining graphite as an allowed solid phase. Across 88 selected Aspen dataset points, the SciPy mean absolute errors were 0.0043 mol H2 and 0.0153 mol carbon per inlet mol CH4. The comparison uses ideal-gas thermodynamics against Aspen's SRK model, so these differences cannot be interpreted as solver accuracy alone. Carbon here means equilibrium propensity, not experimental catalyst coking. Five traceable published optima were also checked separately. DWSIM has not been executed, so I cannot rank it. The code, case manifest, results and limitations will be available after merging: https://github.com/alimardani76/Tri_Reforming_Analysis/tree/main/open_source_benchmark
